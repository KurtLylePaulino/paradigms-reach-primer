# Final image prompts

All project images were generated with the built-in image generation tool. The WebP versions are optimized copies of the selected PNGs.

## Revealed skyline — `assets/reach-skyline.png`

> A tight, condensed telephoto view from above the skyline of Paradigms Reach at deep night: a vast vertical crush of megastructures, angular machine cathedrals, narrow inhabited canyons, tiered elevated rail, smoke stacks, antenna thickets, armoured skybridges, surveillance searchlights, tiny military airships, and dense life far below. The Aegis Spire is a tall blade-like civic fortress slightly right of center, identifiable by a hard scarlet beacon. Post-Convergence machine-magic infrastructure appears as red containment lines and a distant scarred sky. Sharp edges, brutal architectural silhouettes, compressed depth, near-black soot and wet gunmetal, blood-red and rust-red warning light, selective hot white pinlights, dirty rain, and industrial haze. Cinematic, severe, hostile, rich in fine architectural detail, but inhabited. Sci-fi extreme, not medieval fantasy, art deco, generic neon cyberpunk, or purple/blue/cyan. The left third and upper sky remain quieter and darker for live interface text. No text, logos, legible writing, UI, border, or watermark. Wide 16:9 landscape.

## Sealed façade — `assets/sealed-facade.png`

> Use the skyline as a palette and world reference, but hide it behind an extreme close-up of a monumental, sealed, full-frame machine-iron blast façade. A perfect vertical central seam defines left and right armoured leaves that the website can split and move apart. Dense condensed brutalist industrial geometry, razor-sharp angular panels, grime, oil streaks, impact scoring, soot, rivets, armored bracing, narrow scarlet containment conduits, and harsh red warning lights. The fortified outer skin of Paradigms Reach, not a medieval gate or spaceship cockpit. Near-black charcoal and gunmetal dominate; red inner glow leaks from the center seam and hairline fractures. Keep the middle quiet and dark for the live title. Tight symmetrical frontal composition, credible engineering. No skyline yet, figures, text, letters, logos, watermark, decorative sigils, or UI. Wide 16:9 landscape.
